Executive Summary

The design is right. Now make it effortless to buy, and easy to ask.

The approved Art Avenue design already delivers the hard part — a calm, editorial, unmistakably premium brand world. This refinement does not touch that. It threads high-intent actions (WhatsApp consultation, sample requests, customization, consultation booking) and confidence-building merchandising (rug size guidance, material storytelling, shop-by-room, trust cues) through the existing screens so visitors convert without ever feeling sold to.

The thesis

Luxury furniture is a considered, high-ticket, consultative purchase. Our references win not by pushing "Buy now" but by removing doubt — about size, material, delivery and fit — and by making it easy to talk to a human. Every recommendation here serves that.

What changes — and what doesn't

  • Unchanged: palette, type, spacing, photography direction, brand voice.
  • Added: consultative CTAs, rug size guide, merchandising rails, trust modules.
  • Principle: adapt the behaviour of best-in-class stores, never their look.

The four signature additions

1 · "Speak with a Design Consultant" (WhatsApp) on every product page — sticky on mobile, pre-filled with product context. 2 · "Request Fabric / Finish Sample" on furniture & upholstery. 3 · "Need Help Choosing a Rug Size?" → an interactive Rug Size Guide on rug products and collections. 4 · Shop-by-{Room, Size, Material, Colour} merchandising to cut decision fatigue.

Section 02

Principles from the references — adapted, never copied

We study the behaviour these brands have proven, then translate it up into Art Avenue's quieter, more premium register. No visual borrowing.

Anthropologie

What works

  • Lifestyle-led merchandising — products shown in styled rooms, not on white.
  • Editorial "shop the look" and curated edits that sell whole vignettes.
  • Generous, immersive collection headers that set a mood.

Adapt to Art Avenue

  • Lean harder on in-room photography in grids; interleave one lifestyle block per ~6 products.
  • "Shop the Look" module on PDP and room pages — but understated, captioned in italic serif.
  • Keep headers calm: one image, one line, generous air — more restraint than Anthropologie.

Urban Outfitters

What works

  • Frictionless discovery — fast filters, quick-add, infinite-feeling browse.
  • Strong mobile-first interaction patterns and visible social proof.
  • Persistent, low-effort actions (save, quick view).

Adapt to Art Avenue

  • Add quick-view & save-for-later — but slow, elegant transitions, never snappy/loud.
  • Social proof as "Customer Homes" / designer credits, not star-rating clutter.
  • Adopt the speed; reject the density. We show fewer, better things.

Good Earth India

What works

  • Craft & provenance storytelling — the maker, the material, the place.
  • Heritage framing that justifies premium price through meaning.
  • Collections as narratives, not SKUs.

Adapt to Art Avenue

  • Material library + per-product "Materials" and "Craftsmanship" storytelling (already begun — deepen it).
  • Name and narrate collections (e.g. "The Marwar Collection") as we do — extend across all.
  • This is our closest cousin in spirit; out-craft it with cleaner typography and more air.

Guardrail

Art Avenue must read as the most expensive room in the building. Where a reference is busy, we are quiet. Where it shouts a discount, we offer a consultation. Where it shows fifty products, we show twelve and tell their story.

Section 03

Product page enhancements

The PDP is where consideration becomes commitment. Goal: remove every doubt, and make the human one tap away.

High-intent action stack (near price, above the fold)

Beneath price and finish selection, a clear hierarchy of actions — primary purchase, then consultative paths:

  • Add to Cart — primary, charcoal.
  • Speak with a Design Consultant — WhatsApp (see §05). Secondary, with WhatsApp glyph; sticky on mobile.
  • Request Fabric / Finish Sample — on furniture & upholstery. Sends finish + product context.
  • Request Customization — dimensions, finishes, COM.
  • Book a Design Consultation — for room-scale or trade intent.

New CTA — Request Fabric / Finish Sample

On every furniture & upholstery PDP. Lets a buyer feel the material before committing thousands — the single biggest confidence lever for high-ticket, made-to-order furniture. Pre-fills the selected wood finish & upholstery, captures shipping address (or routes to WhatsApp), and is positioned as a complimentary concierge courtesy, not a transaction.

Gallery & material understanding

  • Lifestyle image first, then detail, then material macro, then scale/in-room shot.
  • Thumbnail rail (desktop) + swipe with dots (mobile, built); pinch/hover zoom on detail shots.
  • Optional short looping clip of the material/finish (no autoplay sound, respects reduced-motion).
  • Materials block — named materials with macro imagery and a one-line origin story.
  • Dimensions — labelled line drawing + a "Will it fit?" note and seat-height callouts.
  • Care — concise, reassuring, linked to the Material Library.

Delivery, trust & cross-sell

  • Delivery confidence — secure insured delivery, assembly help on select pieces, lead time, international freight, hand-crating — stated near the buy box, not buried in an accordion.
  • Trust row — Handcrafted in India · Made to order · Customization available · Secure international shipping.
  • Pair With (built) + Shop the Look (the room this piece lives in) — raises AOV.
  • Recently Styled Spaces (built) + Designer Notes — provenance & aspiration.

Section 04

Rug collection experience

Size is the number-one source of hesitation and the number-one driver of regret in rug purchases. A guided, confident size experience is the highest-leverage improvement in the catalogue.

"Need Help Choosing a Rug Size?" — the Rug Size Guide

Entry points
Persistent CTA on every rug PDP (near price) and a banner on rug collection pages; linked from the mega-menu and footer.
Format
Slide-in drawer / modal on PDP (stays in context) + a dedicated, linkable full page for SEO and sharing.
Mobile
Full-height sheet, one room per swipe, large diagrams, thumb-reachable size chips.

Contents

  • Living Room — all-legs-on, front-legs-on, floating layouts.
  • Dining — table + chairs-pulled-out clearance rule.
  • Bedroom — under-bed, runners-either-side, foot-of-bed.
  • Runners — hallways, kitchen, bedside.
  • Recommended sizes per layout (in ft & cm).
  • Placement rules + common mistakes to avoid ("rug too small" is the cardinal sin).

Each layout pairs a clean room diagram with a recommended size and a one-line rationale, then deep-links to "Shop this size". The guide converts browsing into a confident, filtered selection.

Rug merchandising — reduce decision fatigue

On rug collection pages, lead with cross-cutting entry points before the grid:

  • Shop by Size (8×10, 9×12, runners, round…)
  • Shop by Room · Shop by Colour
  • Shop by Pattern · Shop by Material

Present as a quiet row of labelled image tiles (reusing the existing CollectionTile), so a buyer self-selects into a confident, small set rather than scrolling a wall of rugs.

Section 05

WhatsApp enquiry system — specification

A premium, consultative channel — not a support widget. Positioned as access to a design consultant, the way a private gallery offers a personal advisor.

Placement

  • PDP, near Add to Cart — secondary button: "Speak with a Design Consultant" with a discreet WhatsApp glyph.
  • Mobile — included in the sticky bottom action bar so it is always visible without scrolling.
  • Site-wide — an understated floating affordance (bottom-right, bronze, small) on collection & content pages; never a bouncing bubble.
  • Trade & consultation pages — primary path alongside the form.

Pre-filled message

Tapping opens WhatsApp with a context-rich, polite message already composed:

Hello Art Avenue, I'd like to speak with a design consultant about: • Product: {{ product.title }} • SKU: {{ product.sku }} • Link: {{ product.url }} {{ customer_message — default: "I would like more information about this piece." }}

Implementation

Link format
https://wa.me/<NUMBER>?text=<URL-encoded message> (international format, no "+", no spaces).
Context
Inject product title, SKU and canonical URL at render; URL-encode the whole text param.
Number
Single WhatsApp Business number (or click-to-chat). Please provide it — currently a placeholder.
Tone
Consultative copy, serif framing, generous spacing. No "Chat with us!", no emoji, no notification badges.
Tracking
Tag the link with a UTM / event so WhatsApp-sourced enquiries are measurable.

Why it converts

High-ticket, made-to-order buyers want reassurance from a person before committing. A frictionless, pre-contextualised WhatsApp tap collapses "I have a question" into a live, qualified lead — and feels like service, not sales.

Shopify · Prestige implementation plan

The design maps cleanly onto Prestige's section architecture — most of it is theme settings and existing sections; only a few items need light custom Liquid/CSS. Our design tokens drop straight into Prestige's typography & colour settings.

Foundations → theme settings

Colours
Set Prestige colour schemes from tokens/colors.css: page = ivory #FAF6EF, text = charcoal #2B2824, accent = bronze #9C7C4E, dark scheme = espresso #221F1B.
Type
Heading = Cormorant Garamond, Body = Jost (Prestige supports custom fonts; upload licensed faces or use the Google Fonts substitutes documented in the system).
Shape
Corner radius → near-square; buttons → letterspaced uppercase; hairline borders.

Templates → Prestige sections

Custom work (minimise it)

  • WhatsApp CTA — a small product-block snippet building the wa.me link from {{ product.title }}, {{ product.selected_variant.sku }}, {{ shop.url }}{{ product.url }}. URL-encode the text param.
  • Fabric/Finish Sample & Customization — product-form variant, a linked Form page, or WhatsApp (your choice) — gate by product type/tag (e.g. furniture, upholstery).
  • Rug Size Guide — a snippet rendered in a drawer on products tagged rug, plus a standalone page.
  • Merchandising rows / Shop-by-* — linked-collection tiles, no code.
Maintainability
Everything above lives in theme settings + a handful of Liquid blocks — editable by your team, upgrade-safe.
